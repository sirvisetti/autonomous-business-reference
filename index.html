Non-normative reference implementation

From business intent to governed outcome.

Autonomous Business Reference (ABR) demonstrates the governed side of autonomous business: canonical business operations, explicit preconditions, business rules and accountable outcomes that remain consistent whether the requester is an autonomous agent, workflow, application or person.

Try the PlaygroundExplore the Binding
The standard is authoritative. ABR is deliberately non-normative. It demonstrates the current capability contracts and emerging governance pattern while the Autonomous Business Standard defines the normative business semantics.
The governed boundary

Free-form request. Controlled business execution.

Request

An agent, workflow, application or person requests a canonical business operation without dictating the implementation behind it.

Govern

The business operation is evaluated against canonical data, state, preconditions and representative business rules before effects are allowed.

Outcome

The result is explicit: completed or rejected, with the resulting business state and reference metadata available for accountability.

Representative coverage

Three operations. Three governed outcomes.

procurement.purchase-order.submitPreconditionDraft purchase order with valid linesBusiness effectApproval → pending
finance.journal-entry.postPreconditionDraft, balanced journal entryBusiness effectLifecycle → posted
hr.leave-request.submitPreconditionDraft leave request with valid datesBusiness effectSubmitted · approval → pending

These checks are intentionally small but meaningful: the reference shows that autonomy does not imply unconstrained business effects.

Interoperability pattern

Discover. Describe. Invoke.

The reference exposes the current transport-neutral capability model through a tiny optional HTTP binding. Governance belongs to the business operation; the transport and enforcement technology remain implementation choices.

Discover

List the canonical business capabilities this reference surface supports.

GET /capabilities

Describe

Inspect business meaning, preconditions, effects and links to authoritative contracts.

GET /capabilities/{id}

Invoke

Request a canonical business operation and receive an explicit business outcome.

POST /capabilities

Intentionally lightweight

Enough to understand. Not another platform.

ABR contains static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, one serverless function and an OpenAPI description of the optional binding. There is no database, workflow engine, message broker, agent framework or ERP adapter.
