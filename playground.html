Governed Business Playground

Choose a representative business operation, inspect its canonical payload and business guardrails, then invoke the live reference endpoint. The response shows whether the operation is allowed and the canonical business state that results.

This playground starts at the governed execution boundary. It does not simulate agent reasoning or natural-language planning. Any agent, workflow, application or person can arrive at the same canonical business operation; the business rules remain the same.
Live reference endpoint: abr.sirvisetti.com/capabilities
Request an operation to see the governed outcome.
Reference validation: ABR checks representative business preconditions and rules in addition to required canonical fields. The current normative contracts remain the specification and JSON Schemas at abs.sirvisetti.com; the broader business-governance model is being developed for the next normative draft.
